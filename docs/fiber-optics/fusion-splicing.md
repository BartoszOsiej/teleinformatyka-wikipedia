# Fusion Splicing

Permanently joining two optical fibers by melting them together with an electric arc.

---

## Types of Splicing

| Type | Loss | Durability | Cost | Use |
|------|------|-----------|------|-----|
| **Fusion** | 0.02-0.05 dB | Permanent | Equipment cost | Permanent installations |
| **Mechanical** | 0.1-0.5 dB | Semi-permanent | Low | Emergency repairs |

---

## Fusion Splicing Process

1. **Strip** — Remove buffer coating (250μm → 125μm bare fiber)
2. **Clean** — Wipe with lint-free cloth + isopropyl alcohol
3. **Cleave** — Cut fiber end perpendicular (\1° angle)
4. **Align** — Fiber cores aligned (cladding or core alignment)
5. **Splice** — Electric arc melts fibers together
6. **Protect** — Heat-shrink sleeve or splice protector

---

## Splicer Types

| Type | Alignment | Accuracy | Cost |
|------|-----------|----------|------|
| **Core alignment** | Aligns fiber cores | Highest | $5,000-$20,000 |
| **Cladding alignment** | Aligns fiber cladding | Good | $1,000-$5,000 |
| **Ribbon** | Splices 4-12 fibers at once | High | $10,000-$40,000 |

---

## Splice Loss Sources

| Source | Typical Loss |
|--------|-------------|
| Core eccentricity | 0.01-0.03 dB |
| Mode field diameter mismatch | 0.01-0.05 dB |
| Angular misalignment | 0.01-0.02 dB |
| Contamination | 0.1-1.0+ dB |
| Bad cleave angle | 0.05-0.5 dB |

---

## Splice Protector

| Type | Size | Use |
|------|------|-----|
| **Heat-shrink** | 40-60 mm | Standard splice tray |
| **Ribbon** | 40 mm | Ribbon fiber |
| **Mechanical** | Varies | Field repair |

---

## Cleave Quality

| Cleave Angle | Quality | Expected Loss |
|-------------|---------|---------------|
| &lt;1° | Excellent | &lt;0.02 dB |
| 1-3° | Good | 0.02-0.05 dB |
| 3-5° | Marginal | 0.05-0.1 dB |
| &gt;5° | Poor | &gt;0.1 dB (re-cleave) |
